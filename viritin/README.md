# Home Assistant Add-on: Viritin

Brings the internet radio button back to life on AV receivers whose vTuner
service was shut down — Yamaha, Denon, Onkyo, Marantz, Pioneer, Harman Kardon
and others of that generation.

Viritin answers the service the receiver is still trying to reach, so browsing
and playback happen where they always did: on the receiver's own display, with
its own remote. No app, no second box.

- Thousands of stations from [Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info/),
  plus your own list.
- A **Local Stations** entry that goes straight to your country instead of
  scrolling a few hundred of them with a jog dial.
- Playlist links (`.m3u`, `.pls`, `.asx`) unwrapped for firmware that cannot
  follow them.
- Optional podcast feeds, where each show is a folder and each episode plays
  like a station.
- An optional relay for HTTPS-only stations and podcast episodes, which these
  receivers cannot reach on their own.

See [DOCS.md](DOCS.md) for installation, how to point the receiver at it, what
each option does, and the security notes.
